## Publication review

Before public release, inspect screenshots for:

- certificate labels;
- serial numbers;
- distinguished names;
- certificate IDs;
- key-ring names unrelated to the lab;
- internal hostnames;
- email addresses;
- IP/MAC/host adapter details;
- terminal/session identifiers;
- any credential or private-key material.

No private keys should ever be committed.
